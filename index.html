The Reality Creationz Story

Born from a love of masculine fragrance and mood-setting design

Founder Raheem Carter began Reality Creationz to redefine what a candle could be for men who care about atmosphere. What started as small-batch gifts for friends evolved into a full scent lab dedicated to balance—cool, fresh top notes layered over warm, resinous bases.

Every pour is cured for peak performance, hand-labeled, and packaged in vessels meant to complement modern lofts, studios, and lounges. From sourcing ethically crafted fragrance oils to designing reusable glassware, we build experiences that last beyond the burn.
